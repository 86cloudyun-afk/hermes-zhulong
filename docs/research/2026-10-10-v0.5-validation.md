## Practical limits

This finite campaign does not establish 24-hour soak, permanent cloud-task
availability, general code correctness from a text contract, subjective
consciousness or safe autonomous kernel promotion. A real persistent host
needs an outer process manager, retained profile/SQLite/API key and Docker
ownership cleanup. The provided systemd file is a template, not an installed
service on another machine. Unknown executions retain occupancy; even child
exit/owned-container cleanup does not automatically manufacture a per-run
quiescence receipt. Bind disk quota and real mail/calendar integrations remain
outside this release.

Independent review and final verification are recorded separately after the
whole-branch review. The paid result above must not be attributed to a later
fixed head without a corresponding run.
