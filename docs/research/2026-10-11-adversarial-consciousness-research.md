## Newly inspected primary sources

**COGITATE Consortium et al., “Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness,” Nature 642, 133–142 (2025), DOI [10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1).** Full article HTML was obtained with HTTP 200. I inspected the abstract, theoretical predictions, main results, discussion, participant/design methods and limitations. Supplementary PDFs and raw datasets were not downloaded or independently reanalysed.

The reported recruitment pool was 256 human participants across fMRI, MEG and intracranial EEG; analysis populations differ after exclusions and optimization/replication partitioning. The experiment used clearly visible, suprathreshold stimuli varying in category, identity, orientation and duration. It tests competing biological implementation predictions under presumed conscious perception; it is not a consciousness detector validated on unconscious people or machines.

* IIT: the most direct preregistered challenge was absence of the predicted sustained posterior synchronization. Some sustained posterior representation findings supported predictions, but orientation information was not sustained as predicted. Sparse V1/V2 sampling is a material limitation: the reported exploratory connectivity comparison included only 12 V1/V2 electrodes versus 472 PFC electrodes.
* GNWT: the main challenge was failure to find the predicted ignition at stimulus offset, despite detectable offset activity elsewhere and onset activity in PFC. Category decoding in PFC coexisted with absent identity representation and limited orientation representation. A global “content present” summary would obscure this dissociation.
* The **preregistered pairwise phase consistency connectivity analysis supported neither theory**. Subsequent **exploratory** dynamic functional connectivity results supported some GNWT connectivity predictions. These are different analyses; the exploratory result must not silently replace the failed primary test.
* The article explicitly distinguishes mathematical/computational cores from biological implementations: changing a proposed biological implementation could retain the core. Thus, “both theories disproved,” “IIT integration falsified everywhere,” and “a workspace makes Hermes conscious” all exceed these results. Human electrode findings do not establish any AI's experience.

**Jack Lindsey, “Emergent Introspective Awareness in Large Language Models,” Anthropic / Transformer Circuits, 29 October 2025, [full technical article](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/introspection/index.html).** This is a primary technical research report, not represented here as a peer-reviewed publication. Full HTML was obtained with HTTP 200; I inspected definitions, methods, main concept-injection results/controls, discussion and limitations. The separate [Anthropic overview](https://www.anthropic.com/research/introspection) was obtained too, but technical claims here rely on the full report.

The study intervenes on residual-stream activations, testing whether reports depend on the intervention before the concept appears in sampled text. Its proposed criteria distinguish accuracy, causal grounding, internality and metacognitive representation. It expressly says it does **not directly demonstrate** metacognitive representations. Under favorable layer/strength settings, Opus 4/4.1 recognize injected concepts on approximately 20% of trials; failures are the norm. No false positives were observed in 100 no-injection trials for production models in that control, which is a finite-sample observation, not a zero population false-positive rate. Some helpful-only variants yielded false positives. Some apparent detections arise only after the model has emitted the injected word and therefore fail the internality criterion. Emotional elaborations and explanations may remain confabulated even when initial anomaly detection is grounded.

The report uses 50 random concepts for systematic layer sweeps, an LLM judge for response categories, and select favorable configurations for across-model comparisons. Those rates are conditional experimental results, not a general reliability estimate for deployed models. Narrow prompt coverage, artificial perturbations, imperfect semantic purity of concept vectors and confounded cross-model comparisons limit transfer. The authors consider several narrow circuits sufficient alternative explanations; they do not demonstrate a general introspective workspace or phenomenal consciousness.

## What this changes in the interpretation of Hermes

1. A receipt, full packet hash or accessible category field does not establish that **each relevant feature** is available throughout an episode or withdrawn when invalid. Test source identity, revision and validity separately at admission, maintenance and withdrawal. Biological timing values and regions should not be copied into software as putative consciousness requirements.
2. Claims should name the intervention target and measured pathway. Hermes controller state, source freshness and provider neural activations are different levels. A provider reading a Hermes diagnostic packet has external evidence about controller state; it has not thereby introspected its own residual stream. API transcripts cannot reproduce Lindsey's activation experiment.
3. A self-description that becomes accurate only after reading delivered context or prior output is useful auditing, but does not pass the report's internality criterion. Verbal confidence and emotional language cannot supply an experience label.
4. Keep primary endpoint failures, exploratory rescues, missing measurements and positive sensitivity controls visible. These constraints supplement current work; they do not authorize changing frozen hypotheses after seeing v0.11 results.

## One concrete next experiment: feature withdrawal across the actual workspace route

Proposed separate future protocol; **not executed or preregistered by this memo**. Test the finite engineering claim: “For every registered consumer required to use a routed source, source identity, revision and current validity remain usable while admitted, and are no longer usable after authoritative withdrawal.” This tests content-specific transport and update behavior; it is not GNWT validation.

Precommit a deterministic 24-episode fixture set: 2 source identities × 3 revisions × 2 withdrawal causes (TTL expiry/revision revocation) × 2 task-relevance assignments. Within each episode record three checkpoints: initial admission, unchanged valid maintenance, and first refresh after withdrawal. Business input is identical across arms. At each checkpoint require registered consumers to perform a small independent task whose mechanically verified answer depends on identity, revision and validity separately; expose missing-feature responses rather than forcing guessed answers. Include no-withdrawal sham episodes as a separately allocated control set. Define exact consumer list, refresh boundary, fields and complete denominator before execution.

Compare intact delivery, targeted removal of one feature at the consumer boundary, and restoration from the same prior checkpoint. Keep source facts, admitted set, consumer code, capacity and budgets fixed. A separate bypass control deliberately supplies the feature through the business-input path; improvement there establishes that the task/consumer can detect the feature, while demonstrating how alternate routes can defeat a workspace-only causal claim. Run provider-free consumers first to validate transport assertions; any later native-model version needs its own allocations and provider accounting, and has a different claim scope.

Primary finite claim falsifiers: any intact valid checkpoint cannot use a required field; any invalid field is accepted after the first required refresh; any allocated checkpoint disappears; or targeted removal leaves a supposedly route-exclusive feature usable through an unrecorded side channel. Restoration must recover the same finite business answer under matched inputs, not merely a receipt hash. A failed positive sensitivity control makes the null result uninterpretable, not successful. For deterministic fixtures, report exact failures/all allocated checkpoints; do not manufacture inferential generalization from repeated copies.

Success establishes only a bounded, content-sensitive distribution and withdrawal mechanism. Failure rejects that mechanism's finite claim or its purported exclusivity. Neither outcome provides a subjective-consciousness truth label, falsifies consciousness in Hermes, or establishes awareness in a provider model. Activation-level introspection would require a separately authorized model with inspectable/intervenable activations; substituting API self-reports would not supply the missing evidence.
