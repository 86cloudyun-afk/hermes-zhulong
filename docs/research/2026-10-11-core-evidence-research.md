## Consciousness mechanisms

Butlin et al. ([2023 v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.08708v3), AST pp.33–34 and implementation discussion) distinguish a predictive model that represents and enables control over attention from a verbal self-description. Their indicator framework does not yield a validated subjective-experience label. Wilterson and Graziano ([PNAS 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8379943/)) supply an attention-window schema, compare training/ablation conditions and acknowledge a much simpler mechanism than human attention. Liu et al. ([2023 v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.17375v3), §4.6 and §5) use a recurrent attention-output predictor and intervention on attention scores, with placement/control ablations; their preliminary results are not a general consciousness demonstration.

The actionable difference from our baseline is prospective prediction and actual control. The old forecast is a duplicate of its immediate selection calculation. A future-observation ledger must freeze predictions before the target exists, retain unavailable/missed/late cases, and learn only from earlier settled outcomes. The optional controller represents real focus and waiting state, changes eligible-source ranking, and preserves admission and fairness. Source freshness is an auxiliary state model, not a full attention schema. Constant/permuted/restored controls test whether its state matters on discriminating tasks. Actual delivered context remains observable; provider hidden activations and subjective experience remain unidentified.

Fleming and Lau ([2014](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2014.00443)) distinguish metacognitive bias, sensitivity and efficiency. A freshness Brier score has a declared external target; calling it a consciousness or metacognitive-efficiency score would exceed its meaning. IIT 4.0 ([2023](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465)) needs specified units and intervention-defined causal structure. API metadata cannot identify the provider's physical substrate. The scientific status is `subjective_consciousness=not_established`, meaning insufficient evidence rather than demonstrated absence.

## General correctness through an explicit restricted domain

There is no sound, terminating decision procedure that settles arbitrary program behavior. A useful constructive step is to restrict syntax and effects, define the exact business relation, and check a certificate for every admitted input. [Lean's introduction](https://lean-lang.org/theorem_proving_in_lean4/Introduction/) separates proof objects from their construction; its [axioms discussion](https://lean-lang.org/theorem_proving_in_lean4/Axioms-and-Computation/) distinguishes kernel checking from compiled computation. [Dafny's termination tutorial](https://dafny.org/dafny/OnlineTutorial/Termination) makes decreasing measures explicit. These are design sources; no Lean/Dafny compiler was installed or run in this research.

For the existing ticket-priority task, a bounded typed AST is total by structural evaluation. A gap-free integer interval partition covers all 876,010 severity/age/blocked rows. On each interval, derive affine arithmetic and prove branch uniformity from exact extrema, then check independent score/priority relations. Candidate witnesses cannot choose their own domain or specification. The protected fixed list wrapper preserves each unique ID and orders all records. Alternative equivalent ASTs are allowed, while IO, recursive calls and arbitrary Python are excluded from the language.

Actual execution must use the same canonical AST and certificate bytes that passed checking, bound to the trusted frozen contract and authorized input. Hashes alone do not show execution or correctness. The bridge must independently record interpreter/program/certificate/input/output hashes, and the existing business artifact verifier still decides completion. The checker argument is reviewed mathematical reasoning; until the exact checker/interpreter/wrapper theorem is mechanically formalized, it must not be advertised as machine-proven soundness. Exhaustive numeric cross-checks supplement implementation validation and do not turn arbitrary Python into certified code.

## Long-term benefit needs execution, identification and time

Hernán and Robins ([Causal Inference: What If](https://miguelhernan.org/whatifbook), randomized assignment and sustained strategies) motivate explicit units, consistency, intervention and complete original denominators. Dwan et al. ([CONSORT crossover extension, BMJ 2019](https://www.bmj.com/content/366/bmj.l4378)) emphasize sequence, period effects, carryover and paired analysis. Clinical assumptions are not imported as facts about agents; fresh isolated profiles and source/input matching are engineering controls. Howard et al. ([2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08240)) distinguish time-uniform inference from fixed-sample estimates repeatedly inspected for stopping. Our fixed-N protocol cannot gain an anytime-valid guarantee by repeated summaries.

Kapoor et al. ([AI Agents That Matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01502)) motivate held-out execution, cost control and reproducibility. [AgentBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03688) and [SWE-bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770) motivate mechanically assessed task outcomes with bounded generalization. Complete actual spending includes main, guardian/auxiliary, preparation and hidden retries; incomplete counters remain unknown.

The immediate study isolates only planner workspace visibility across three fixture families, with six actual native allocations. It can verify exposure, binding, independent business outcomes and cleanup; it is too small and too easy to establish general causal benefit. A null result is retained. The existing conservative effect interval has radius sqrt(2 log(2/alpha)/N); at alpha=.05, N=30 gives about .496, N=512 about .120. N>=738 only makes the radius smaller than .10 and is not a power guarantee.

Durable cohorts separately preregister actual observation windows, state lineage, launch intent, ownership and recovery. A final observation 72 hours later establishes elapsed follow-up, not uninterrupted service. Continuous liveness requires a cadence and bounded gaps; causal long-term usefulness requires work outcomes and appropriate matched units. Simulated lifecycle clocks, successful unit tests and an unlimited research budget cannot supply missing elapsed time or causal assumptions.
